Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Welcome to the FAQs section for Stock Manager TT, a simple and powerful tool designed for small businesses in Trinidad and Tobago.

📦 General Questions

1. Is Stock Manager TT free to use?

✅ Yes! Stock Manager TT is completely free to use. You can access it directly from your browser — no installation required.

2. Do I need an internet connection to use it?

🔌 The app works best with an internet connection, especially when exporting or importing data. You can also save it as a PWA and use it offline, but some features may be limited.

3. Can I use this app on mobile devices?

📱 Yes! The app is fully responsive and works on desktops, tablets, and smartphones. You can install it as a web app on your home screen for easy access.

4. Is my data secure?

🔒 All your data is stored locally in your browser’s localStorage. It is not sent to any server unless you manually export it.

Note: For sensitive business data, always ensure your device is secure and back up regularly.

📥 Importing Data

1. What file formats does the app support for import?

📥 Supported formats include:

  • .csv (Comma-Separated Values)
  • .xlsx, .xls (Excel files)
  • .ods (OpenDocument Spreadsheets)

2. How do I prepare my CSV file for import?

Your CSV should contain these columns:

Column Description
Name Product name
Supplier Optional: Supplier name
Barcode Unique ID for the product
Quantity Number of items in stock
Price Cost per unit
Last Updated Date/time of last update

The column "Total Value" will be ignored during import.

3. Why isn’t my CSV file importing correctly?

🛠️ Common issues:

  • Missing required fields (Name, Barcode, Quantity, Price)
  • Duplicate barcodes
  • Extra characters like $, ", or commas
  • Incorrect formatting

The app automatically skips invalid rows and duplicates.

4. Can I undo an import if something goes wrong?

🔄 Yes! Use the "Undo Last Import" button to restore your previous product list.

📤 Exporting Data

1. How do I export my inventory list?

💾 Click the "Export to CSV" button to download your current inventory as a CSV file.

This file includes:

  • Product Name
  • Supplier
  • Barcode
  • Quantity
  • Price
  • Total Value (calculated as Quantity × Price)
  • Last Updated timestamp
  • A final row showing the Grand Total value of all stock

2. Does the exported CSV include dollar signs?

💰 No. Prices and total values are exported without $ symbols for easier use in other systems.

3. Can I edit the exported CSV and re-import it?

📝 Yes! You can:

  1. Export your inventory
  2. Edit it in Excel or Google Sheets
  3. Save it as .csv
  4. Re-import it into the app

Important: Don’t change or remove the barcode field to avoid duplicates.

⚙️ Managing Products & Suppliers

1. How do I add a new supplier?

➕ To add a supplier:

  1. Type the supplier's name into the input field under “Add Supplier”
  2. Click the "Add Supplier" button

Note: Duplicate supplier names are not allowed.

2. Can I edit product quantity or price after adding them?

✍️ Yes! You can edit the quantity or price directly in the table — changes are saved automatically.

3. How do I delete a product?

🗑️ Each product has a "Delete" button in the last column. You’ll be prompted to confirm deletion.

4. Can I search for products?

🔍 Yes! Use the search bar at the top to find products by product name, supplier, or barcode. Results update in real time.

🔍 Low Stock Tracking

1. How does low stock tracking work?

📉 You can set a low stock threshold using the input box under "Low Stock Warning". Any product with quantity equal to or less than that number will show a warning message.

2. How do I view low stock items only?

🔎 Click the "Show Low Stock" button to filter and display only products that are running low.

🔄 Undo & Reset

1. Can I undo changes or imports?

↩️ Yes! You can:

  • Undo Last Change: Reverts to the previous version of your product list
  • Undo Last Import: Restores your data to what it was before the most recent import

Warning: These actions cannot be undone once confirmed.

2. How do I clear all products?

💣 Click "Clear Product List" to remove all products from the app. A backup is saved so you can restore later if needed.

3. Can I reset the entire app?

🔄 Yes! Click "Reset App" to remove all data including products, suppliers, settings, and backups. Use this option carefully.

🛠️ Technical Help

1. Why am I seeing errors when importing?

🚨 Possible causes:

  • Invalid file format
  • Missing required columns
  • Extra characters like $, ", or commas
  • Duplicates based on barcode

The app will skip problematic rows and notify how many products were successfully imported.

2. Can I customize the app or add more features?

🔧 Yes! The app is open-source and customizable. You can:

  • Add product categories
  • Track expiry dates
  • Set reorder level alerts
  • Build a multi-user version

Let me know if you'd like help building any advanced features!
