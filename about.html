About Us – Stock Manager TT

Welcome to Stock Manager TT, a simple and powerful tool designed for small businesses in Trinidad and Tobago who need an easy way to manage inventory, track products, and stay organized — no matter how big or small your operation is.

👥 Who We Are

We are a team of local developers and entrepreneurs passionate about helping small businesses thrive through technology. With years of experience in web development and business operations, we created Stock Manager TT as a free, open-source solution that helps you:

  • Track products
  • Manage suppliers
  • Monitor stock levels
  • Import and export data easily

Whether you run a grocery store, restaurant, or online shop, this tool helps you stay on top of your inventory — with no complicated setup or cost.

💡 Why We Built This Tool

Managing inventory can be frustrating — especially when you're relying on outdated methods like spreadsheets or pen-and-paper systems.

That’s why we created Stock Manager TT — to give you a better way to manage your business from your browser, with no fees and no complex software.

Our goal is to provide tools that are:

  • ✅ Simple to use
  • ✅ Free to access
  • ✅ Designed with Trinidad & Tobago businesses in mind

🛠️ What It Does

Stock Manager TT lets you:

  • ✅ Add and manage products with barcode, quantity, and price
  • ✅ Track suppliers and assign them to products
  • ✅ Set low stock thresholds and get alerts when items are running low
  • ✅ Search products by name, supplier, or barcode
  • ✅ Export your inventory list as a CSV file
  • ✅ Import product lists from Excel or CSV files
  • ✅ Undo changes or restore previous versions
  • ✅ Clear or reset the app anytime

All data is stored locally in your browser — no servers, no fees, no subscriptions.

🌐 For Trinidad & Tobago Businesses

We understand the unique challenges faced by small businesses here at home — from supply chain issues to limited digital tools. That's why we've designed this tool to be accessible, lightweight, and useful for real-world situations.

You don’t need technical expertise to use it — just open the app, start adding products, and begin managing your inventory like a pro.
